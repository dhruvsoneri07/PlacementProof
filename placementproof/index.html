Why this matters

Good advice is simple when a message feels urgent.

Final-year students and first-time job seekers may receive notices through forwards, screenshots or group chats. The original source can disappear, while an impersonated employer may ask for application, training or background-check payments.

Context, not a fraud-rate claim. CERT-In’s 2024 advisory CIAD-2024-0050 describes fake job listings aimed at fresh graduates. A 2025 Ministry of Rural Development / PIB report described a fake NRDRM recruitment website removed through I4C and followed by an FIR.
