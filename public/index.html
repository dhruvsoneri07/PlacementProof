Threat Landscape & SDG 8

Why provenance matters in recruitment cybersecurity.

Fresh graduates and first-time job seekers are prime targets for employment scam syndicates. Fraudsters frequently harvest legitimate company names, set up lookalike websites, and forward tampered notices with upfront fees disguised as "laptop deposits" or "aptitude test fees".

Advisory References: CERT-In Advisory CIAD-2024-0050 details widespread fake recruitment campaigns aimed at Indian graduates. A 2025 Ministry of Rural Development / PIB report identified malicious impersonation sites taken down via I4C coordination following FIR registrations.
